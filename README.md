## MVP Overview

MindShift Manifest is designed to guide users through a consistent, structured daily practice that combines:
- conscious self‑observation
- belief reprogramming
- somatic calming
- intentional focus on desired outcomes

All features work together to help users:
- shift core beliefs and self‑talk
- build new, aligned habits
- maintain emotional regulation
- consistently direct attention toward manifestations they care about

This document focuses on **what the current MVP lets a user do**, **how each feature feels to use**, and **how it supports mindset transformation and manifestation goals**.

## Conscious Dashboard

**Purpose**
- Provide a single, grounded starting point for each session.
- Reflect back the user’s current “vibration” and practice consistency.
- Surface the most important next actions without overwhelming the user.

**User Actions & Workflow**
- Land on the dashboard after login.
- See an at‑a‑glance **vibration score**, current **streak**, and recent activity.
- Review a **daily affirmation** that is generated from their own data (beliefs, goals, logs).
- Jump directly into the next relevant practice: journaling, reframing, breathing, rituals.

**Key UI Elements**
- Vibration score indicator (0–100) derived from:
  - streak length
  - completed FTBA entries
  - gratitude items
  - breathing sessions
  - whisper goals / manifestations
  - belief work
- Daily affirmation card with:
  - short, clear statement aligned to current focus
  - context pulled from their stored data
- Quick links / tiles to core practices (Journaling, 3‑6‑9, 5‑5‑5, Release, etc.).

**Impact on Mindset & Manifestation**
- Makes “inner state” visible and trackable, instead of vague or purely emotional.
- Reinforces a feedback loop: **actions → higher vibration score → motivation to continue**.
- Keeps the user oriented toward their active manifestations via tailored affirmations.

## Belief Reframer

**Purpose**
- Help users identify limiting beliefs and actively rewrite them into empowering alternatives.
- Connect emotional triggers to underlying narratives and new, chosen meanings.

**User Actions & Workflow**
- Add a new belief entry by:
  - capturing the **trigger situation** (what happened)
  - writing the **automatic thought** or belief that arose
  - identifying the **felt emotion** and intensity
- Use guided prompts to:
  - challenge the old belief (evidence for/against)
  - rewrite it into a new empowering belief
  - craft a concise reframed statement / affirmation.
- Mark beliefs as **active**, **integrating**, or **resolved** over time.

**Key UI Elements**
- List of beliefs with:
  - title (short belief summary)
  - status / stage
  - tags (e.g. self‑worth, money, relationships).
- Detail pane for each belief containing:
  - original belief and context
  - reframed belief
  - notes or evidence logs.

**Impact on Mindset & Manifestation**
- Transforms vague “I know I have limiting beliefs” into **specific, trackable cognitive shifts**.
- Repeated exposure to reframed beliefs builds new mental defaults.
- Directly supports manifestation by aligning subconscious narratives with desired outcomes.

## FTBA Journaling (Feel → Thought → Belief → Action)

**Purpose**
- Make daily emotional experiences a structured training ground for new responses.
- Help users see how feelings lead to thoughts, beliefs, and actions—and how to intervene.

**User Actions & Workflow**
- Start a new FTBA entry:
  - **Feel**: describe current emotion and intensity.
  - **Thought**: capture spontaneous thoughts (“what my mind is saying”).
  - **Belief**: surface the underlying assumption or story.
  - **Action**: choose or design a more aligned behavior.
- Save entries, then:
  - filter by emotion, tag, or date
  - revisit past entries to track how reactions and stories evolve.

**Key UI Elements**
- FTBA form with four clearly separated sections.
- List view of past entries with:
  - quick summary (emotion + main belief)
  - timestamp and tags.
- Filters to focus on specific patterns (e.g. anxiety, money, relationships).

**Impact on Mindset & Manifestation**
- Builds self‑awareness: users see recurring stories and emotional loops.
- Creates a pattern of **conscious action selection** instead of automatic reactions.
- Over time, the user learns that challenging situations are opportunities for reprogramming.

## Gratitude & Wins Log

**Purpose**
- Shift attention toward what is already working, abundant, and aligned.
- Train the brain to notice evidence that supports the user’s desired reality.

**User Actions & Workflow**
- Add gratitude items (people, experiences, resources, internal qualities).
- Log daily “wins” or micro‑successes, including:
  - progress on goals
  - synchronicities
  - signs that manifestations are unfolding.
- Review past entries to see how far they’ve come.

**Key UI Elements**
- Simple input for new gratitude or win items.
- List of past entries with dates.
- Optional tags (e.g. health, career, relationships).

**Impact on Mindset & Manifestation**
- Increases positive affect and resilience.
- Reinforces a belief that “things are working out for me”.
- Provides concrete evidence of forward movement even when big outcomes are still forming.

## Whisper Goals (Intentional Manifestation Targets)

**Purpose**
- Translate vague desires into clear, emotionally resonant goals.
- Keep active manifestations visible and connected to daily practices.

**User Actions & Workflow**
- Create whisper goals by specifying:
  - what they want (clear, specific description)
  - why it matters (emotional driver)
  - how they will feel when it arrives.
- Mark goals as **active**, **in progress**, or **complete**.
- Reference whisper goals when:
  - journaling (to reframe stories around them)
  - running rituals (3‑6‑9, 5‑5‑5, Ho‘oponopono)
  - reading daily affirmations on the dashboard.

**Key UI Elements**
- Goal cards listing:
  - title, category, current status.
- Detail view with:
  - expanded description
  - emotional anchors
  - optional checkpoints or milestones.

**Impact on Mindset & Manifestation**
- Encourages clarity and emotional alignment around what the user is calling in.
- Links goals to repeated practices, making manifestation feel **active and embodied**, not passive.

## 3‑6‑9 Manifestation Tracker

**Purpose**
- Support the classic 3‑6‑9 scripting pattern in a structured, trackable way.
- Deepen focus on a specific intention across the morning, afternoon, and evening.

**User Actions & Workflow**
- Choose a focus intention (often from a whisper goal).
- For each day:
  - **3x (morning)**: write or review the intention three times.
  - **6x (mid‑day)**: revisit and deepen the wording or emotion.
  - **9x (evening)**: close the day with an extended, aligned script.
- Track how consistently they complete all three phases.

**Key UI Elements**
- Day card showing:
  - 3, 6, 9 slots with completion indicators.
- Text inputs or prompts for each phase, emphasizing:
  - clarity of the desire
  - emotional charge
  - gratitude in advance.

**Impact on Mindset & Manifestation**
- Repeated, spaced focus makes the intention “stick” in the user’s awareness.
- The three daily touches create a **beginning–middle–end** narrative for each day.
- Over time, strengthens the expectation that the desired reality is unfolding.

## 5‑5‑5 Repetition Ritual

**Purpose**
- Offer a rapid‑fire repetition practice to saturate the mind with a chosen statement.
- Pair conscious intention with somatic rhythm (typing, speaking, or focusing).

**User Actions & Workflow**
- Select or write a short, powerful statement (e.g. “I am safe and supported”).
- Repeat it across a 5‑5‑5 structure (for example: sets, lines, or timed rounds).
- Optionally pair with breath or simple movement while repeating.

**Key UI Elements**
- Ritual view with:
  - current statement prominently displayed.
  - repetition counters or progress indicators.
- Controls to:
  - start/reset a repetition round
  - mark a ritual as complete.

**Impact on Mindset & Manifestation**
- Uses repetition to reinforce new beliefs at a subconscious level.
- Helps override intrusive or limiting internal dialogue with a chosen narrative.
- Creates a sense of energetic momentum toward the user’s manifestations.

## Release & Transmute (Burning / Letting Go)

**Purpose**
- Give users a safe container to express and release heavy thoughts and emotions.
- Symbolically “burn” what no longer serves, clearing space for new patterns.

**User Actions & Workflow**
- Write out what they want to release:
  - resentments, fears, past stories, self‑criticism, or outdated identities.
- Activate a **burn / release** interaction:
  - watch the text visually fade, shrink, or disappear to symbolize letting go.
- Optionally add a short **integration note**:
  - what they choose to believe or focus on instead.

**Key UI Elements**
- Text area for uncensored writing.
- Animated release interaction (e.g. burn, dissolve, or fade).
- Confirmation state indicating that the entry has been released.

**Impact on Mindset & Manifestation**
- Provides a ritualized way to process and move through emotional weight.
- Reinforces the idea that the user can **consciously end** stories that conflict with their desires.
- Opens emotional bandwidth for new, aligned beliefs and experiences.

## Ho‘oponopono Breathing & Mantra

**Purpose**
- Calm the nervous system and soften resistance using rhythmic breath.
- Integrate the Ho‘oponopono mantra: “I’m sorry, please forgive me, thank you, I love you.”

**User Actions & Workflow**
- Start a guided breathing session.
- Follow on‑screen cues through phases like:
  - **Inhale**, **Hold**, **Exhale**.
- Recite or mentally repeat the Ho‘oponopono phrases in sync with the breath.
- Complete a set number of cycles, then optionally log the session.

**Key UI Elements**
- Central breathing circle that:
  - expands/contracts with the current phase (e.g. inhale, hold, exhale).
  - shifts opacity to signal pacing.
- Phase labels and timer / cycle counts.
- Mantra text displayed to guide the user.

**Impact on Mindset & Manifestation**
- Reduces physiological stress, making it easier to accept new beliefs and possibilities.
- Encourages self‑forgiveness and compassion, dissolving inner resistance to receiving.
- Pairs energetic clearing with an embodied, calming practice.

## Visualization & Future Self

**Purpose**
- Anchor the user’s imagination in a specific, embodied vision of their desired future.
- Connect logical goals with sensory and emotional imagery.

**User Actions & Workflow**
- Create a visualization script describing:
  - where they are
  - what they see, hear, and feel
  - who they are being in that future state.
- Optionally add key **anchors**, such as:
  - symbols
  - scenes
  - phrases that instantly evoke the desired reality.
- Revisit scripts regularly and note insights or shifts.

**Key UI Elements**
- Text fields for guided prompts (environment, emotions, identity, relationships).
- List of saved visualizations with titles and dates.

**Impact on Mindset & Manifestation**
- Strengthens emotional familiarity with the desired reality.
- Encourages behavior that matches the “future self” identity in the present moment.
- Helps bridge the gap between current circumstances and the manifested outcome.

## Streaks, Vibration Score & Progress Signals

**Purpose**
- Turn inner work into something trackable and game‑like.
- Motivate consistent practice, which is essential for real change.

**User Actions & Workflow**
- Earn streak days by:
  - returning to the app on consecutive days
  - completing entries and rituals.
- Watch the **vibration score** shift as they:
  - add FTBA entries
  - log gratitude
  - complete breathing sessions and rituals
  - work with beliefs and goals.
- Use these signals to decide where to focus next (e.g. more journaling vs. more calming).

**Key UI Elements**
- Streak counter showing how many days in a row the user has been active.
- Vibration score meter and supporting metrics.
- Daily affirmation reflecting current patterns and priorities.

**Impact on Mindset & Manifestation**
- Reinforces the link between **consistent inner work** and how the user feels day‑to‑day.
- Encourages small, repeatable actions over sporadic, intense efforts.
- Makes it easier to see progress that might otherwise be invisible or subtle.

## How the MVP Supports Mindset Transformation End‑to‑End

- **Awareness** – FTBA journaling and belief work reveal hidden stories and patterns.
- **Regulation** – Ho‘oponopono breathing and release rituals help calm the nervous system.
- **Reframing** – Belief Reframer, affirmations, and 5‑5‑5 repetition install new narratives.
- **Alignment** – Whisper goals, 3‑6‑9 scripting, and visualization align daily focus with desired outcomes.
- **Reinforcement** – Streaks, vibration score, gratitude, and wins keep attention on progress.

Together, these MVP features create a daily practice that:
- transforms internal dialogue
- stabilizes emotional state
- directs energy and attention toward chosen manifestations
- makes personal growth **observable, actionable, and repeatable**.
